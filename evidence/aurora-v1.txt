Aurora Protocol - Token Allocation and Vesting (version 1)

Total supply: 1,000,000,000 AUR.

Team allocation: the core team receives 15% of the total supply.
Cliff: team tokens have a 12 month cliff, so nothing vests during the first year.
Vesting: after the cliff, team tokens vest linearly over 36 months.
Initial unlock: 5% of the team allocation is unlocked at token generation.
Lock: all team tokens are locked in a vesting smart contract that the team cannot upgrade.
