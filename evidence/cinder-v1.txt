Cinder Labs - Token Allocation and Vesting (version 1)

Total supply: 2,000,000,000 CIN.

Team allocation: the team receives 25% of the total supply.
Cliff: team tokens have a 2 month cliff.
Vesting: after the cliff, team tokens vest linearly over 6 months.
Initial unlock: 5% of the team allocation is unlocked at token generation.
Lock: team tokens are locked in a vesting smart contract.
