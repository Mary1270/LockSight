Borealis Network - Token Allocation and Vesting (version 1)

Total supply: 500,000,000 BOR.

Team allocation: the team receives 18% of the total supply.
Cliff: team tokens have a 9 month cliff.
Vesting: after the cliff, team tokens vest linearly over 30 months.
Initial unlock: 5% of the team allocation is unlocked at token generation.
Lock: team tokens are held in escrow by a third-party custodian.
