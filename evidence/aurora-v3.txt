Aurora Protocol - Token Allocation and Vesting (version 3)

Supersedes: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Mary1270/LockSight/main/evidence/aurora-v2.txt

Total supply: 1,000,000,000 AUR.

Team allocation: the core team receives 20% of the total supply.
Cliff: team tokens have a 6 month cliff.
Vesting: after the cliff, team tokens vest linearly over 24 months.
Initial unlock: 5% of the team allocation is unlocked at token generation.
Lock: team tokens are locked in a vesting smart contract.
